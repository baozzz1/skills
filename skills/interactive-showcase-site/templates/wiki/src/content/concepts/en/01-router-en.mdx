<h2 id="problem">Problem</h2>

One interactive shape cannot serve every technical story. The router prevents a static explainer, a timeline, a card collection, and a graph atlas from collapsing into one muddy template.

<h2 id="structure">Structure</h2>

The decision starts with the reader's movement model: scan one page, follow one timeline, browse peer objects, or navigate a relationship map.

<h2 id="implementation">Implementation</h2>

`SKILL.md` names the archetype, explains why it fits, and waits for confirmation before copying a scaffold.

<h2 id="when-not-to-use">When not to use</h2>

Do not route by visual taste alone. If the relationships are the core idea, cards are the wrong answer even when the objects look collectible.
