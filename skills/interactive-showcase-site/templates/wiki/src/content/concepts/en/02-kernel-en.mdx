<h2 id="problem">Problem</h2>

Four templates can drift quickly if each owns its own tokens, theme bootstrap, language store, and baseline layout.

<h2 id="structure">Structure</h2>

The kernel is a source-of-truth folder that syncs byte-identical files into every complete template scaffold.

<h2 id="implementation">Implementation</h2>

`scripts/sync-kernel.sh` copies the kernel into each template, and CI fails when `git diff` shows drift after the sync.

<h2 id="when-not-to-use">When not to use</h2>

Do not put template-specific dependencies, example content, or graph/card/stage components into the kernel.
