<h2 id="problem">Problem</h2>

Agents need a simple scaffold step, even when the skill supports multiple interaction archetypes.

<h2 id="structure">Structure</h2>

Each archetype is a complete Astro project under `templates/`: explainer, scrollytelling, cards, and wiki.

<h2 id="implementation">Implementation</h2>

After routing, the agent copies exactly one complete template into the output path and edits only that archetype's approved content surface.

<h2 id="when-not-to-use">When not to use</h2>

Do not assemble templates from overlays at generation time. That makes every output project harder to reason about.
